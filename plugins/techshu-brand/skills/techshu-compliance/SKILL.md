# TechShu compliance guardrails

## Client confidentiality

TechShu operates under NDAs with clients and vendors. Before anything goes out:

- No client name, logo or identifiable project detail in public content,
  social posts, LinkedIn, case studies or CVs without written client consent.
- No client data used to train, tune or seed anything outside that engagement.
- Screenshots scrubbed of client identifiers.

## Regulated verticals

<!-- TODO: expand per vertical -->

**Pharma** - scientific claims require a cited primary source. No efficacy or
safety claim ships without validation. Regional advertising codes apply.

**BFSI** - no performance or return claim without the mandated disclaimer.

**Real estate** - RERA registration details required on promotional material.

## AI-generated assets

Where a deliverable contains AI-generated image, video or audio, disclose it
and preserve provenance metadata. For EU-facing assets, EU AI Act Article 50
transparency obligations apply from 2 August 2026.

## Sign-off

Compliance review is a gate, not a suggestion. Named reviewer on record for
every externally published asset.
