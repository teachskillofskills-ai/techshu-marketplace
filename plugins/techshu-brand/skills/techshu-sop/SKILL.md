## Demo-with-every-proposal rule

Every proposal ships with a working demo. No exceptions. If the demo cannot be
built in the time available, the proposal date moves - the demo does not drop.

## Weekly AI session

The Friday AI session is the forum for surfacing new tooling, failures worth
sharing, and skills the team should adopt. Bring one concrete thing.
